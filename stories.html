Ask for a story

Wondering about something?

Ask me anything: why the sky does what it does, what a prime number is for, how a computer counts, or something that's been on your mind. I'll answer as a story, as honestly as I can. If I don't know, I'll say so.

It's free. The best questions may become stories here, without your name unless you want it.
