Research

Abhijit Singh

One principle read across number theory, geometry and the physical sciences.

Objects are related only through the mechanism of their construction.

The work studies what binds two objects that one construction builds: the two members of a twin prime pair, the forward and reversed terms of the zeta function, the two strands of a finite structure. Its centre is a small object, the medial antiprism, in which every step passes only a sum, and its two strands meet only at one half.

    The structure

    The medial antiprism

    Triangles are stacked along an axis, each turned through $180^\circ$ against the one below, and every vertex is joined to every side midpoint of the neighbouring layers. The side midpoints of a triangle are its vertices multiplied by $-\tfrac12$, so one move, a half-turn with a halving, builds the whole object.

    Three games on one structure and its arithmetic, each run only on its own rule, with every number beside it counted as it runs. In the 6D cosmos, the six-layer structure holds itself in every node, scale inside scale, and a current from the scale above flows down through every scale at once. In the world of clocks, prime clocks mark the pairs $6k\pm1$, and what no clock marks is a twin prime pair. In the third, triad spins sit on a strand, and order travels along the axis as far as one coupling lets it.

    Drag to turn, scroll to zoom towards the pointer, right-drag to move. Nothing of the structure is drawn. Light comes only from the points where two lines meet, as much as the current exchanged there. A node seen from far away is one object holding all the light inside it; as you come near enough to resolve it, it opens into the structure within, through all six scales. Colour is the rate at which a place turns against the frequency arriving from the scale above: white where it turns with it, one turn of the colour wheel for each halving below it. The bars give what an observer inside could measure: the rate of the scale above, the current now, the energy with what has come in from above and gone out to the endless structure within, how many meetings are exchanging, and for each scale its sum as a share of the whole (what it passes on), its temperature (the spread of its currents) and its current.

    Scale 1 is the structure: six layers, each the one before turned by −1, each with a triad of vertices and a triad of midpoints, 36 nodes. Every node of scale 1 is the whole structure within, which is scale 2, and so on to scale 6: 366 = 2,176,782,336 vertices. The structure within is the same in every node of a scale, so six structures of 36 carry them all. Nothing is put in. Every node begins at the turn where it sits, so the current carries the structure from the start. The scale above, which holds the whole, turns at the rate of lightning, the current at which a layer turns by itself, and is joined to every node of scale 1. Every node of each scale is joined to every node of the structure within it, so what crosses between scales is their sums, and no scale sees more of another than its sum. The structure within scale 6 is never reached; it takes whatever arrives. Every bridge and every meeting pulls toward the coherence of the triad, (1 + 2 cos d)/3. So the current flows one way, from the scale above down through the six, and is never zero everywhere at once. Begun from still phases instead of the structure's turns, the scale above only rocks the whole together and nothing emerges.

    No training

    Reading images and language with no training

    Two architectures from the triad: a pair read forward and in reverse, the middle that stays, and the turn between the two ends. Nothing is fitted by gradient descent and no network weights exist; each writes its memory in one pass and is judged on held-out data beside a scrambled control. Code, data and every run: github.com/mag-and-krotons/triad-pattern.

    The six-image stack

    Three images a, b, c of a digit are read forward, (a − b) − c, and in reverse, (c − b) − a. The sum of the two readings is the middle, −2b; their difference is the turn, 2(a − c). Each digit keeps its still pattern and its own turning directions. A new image is read as the digit whose still pattern, allowed to turn in that digit’s directions, leaves the least behind.

    Still pattern only: 89.8%. Written two digits at a time and never shown again: 97.71%. Labels scrambled: 10.8%.

    The triad flow

    The text is a flow through the 26 letters and silence. A letter is the middle of its triads (x−k, x, x+k): the pair of flows into it and out of it, and the turn, the two ends counted together. The triads stop at silence, what flowed returns, and the word triad is the same reading one scale up.

    The same 6 letters read as a context before the letter: 51.8%. Read as three triads around it: 81.0%. Scrambled text: 11.7%.

    My research reads one principle in many settings: objects are related only through the mechanism of their construction. In number theory it gives the exclusion law of twin primes and the axiom of harmonic independence for the zeros of the zeta function. In geometry it is the medial antiprism, whose two strands meet only at one half. In physics, chemistry, the nerve and the giant planets it appears as the pair, the triad and the half.

    The complete research release 8.0.2 was published on Zenodo on 27 August 2026 and is archived in Software Heritage.
