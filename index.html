Research

Abhijit Singh

One principle read across number theory, geometry and the physical sciences.

Objects are related only through the mechanism of their construction.

The work studies what binds two objects that one construction builds: the two members of a twin prime pair, the forward and reversed terms of the zeta function, the two strands of a finite structure. Its centre is a small object, the medial antiprism, in which every step passes only a sum, and its two strands meet only at one half.

    The medial antiprism

    Triangles are stacked along an axis, each turned through $180^\circ$ against the one below, and every vertex is joined to every side midpoint of the neighbouring layers. The side midpoints of a triangle are its vertices multiplied by $-\tfrac12$, so one move, a half-turn with a halving, builds the whole object.

    Three games on one structure and its arithmetic, each run only on its own rule, with every number beside it counted as it runs. In the 6D cosmos, the six-layer structure holds itself in every node, scale inside scale, and every node takes one count across its bridges at every scale at once. In the world of clocks, prime clocks mark the pairs $6k\pm1$, and what no clock marks is a twin prime pair. In the third, triad spins sit on a strand, and order travels along the axis as far as one coupling lets it.

    Drag to turn, scroll to zoom towards the pointer, right-drag to move. Nothing of the structure is drawn. Light comes only from the points where two lines of the structure meet, as much as the current exchanged there. The observer keeps a record of how fast each place turns. Where a place turns faster than the cosmos as a whole, its light takes a colour, one turn of the colour wheel for each doubling; where it turns at the common rate the light is white. The bars give what an observer inside could measure: the current put in and the current now (they stay equal), the energy (it stays the same), how many meeting points are exchanging, the current crossing each gap between the six layers of the outermost level (its one-way part, then its alternating part), each layer's strongest frequency and its temperature (the spread of its places' currents), and how the motion is shared among the six layers, with the share held by the busiest hundredth of places.

    Every node of the six layers holds the whole structure again, halved and turned half a turn, so a node of this cosmos is a node at every level at once. Each node holds a pair: its phase and its current, the rate at which the phase turns. Every bridge, and every point where two bridges cross, pulls toward the coherence of the triad, (1 + 2 cos d)/3, and the pull moves current from one end to the other, so the total current and the energy are both kept. The step runs the same backwards as forwards; nothing in it counts time. The cosmos begins still, the current is put in once, into one layer, and nothing is touched afterwards. Without the crossings the two strands never meet and a strike reaches only its own strand; with them, one strike reaches every node at every level.

    My research reads one principle in many settings: objects are related only through the mechanism of their construction. In number theory it gives the exclusion law of twin primes and the axiom of harmonic independence for the zeros of the zeta function. In geometry it is the medial antiprism, whose two strands meet only at one half. In physics, chemistry, the nerve and the giant planets it appears as the pair, the triad and the half.

    The complete research release 8.0.2 was published on Zenodo on 27 August 2026 and is archived in Software Heritage.
