            BIOGRAPHY & RESEARCH SCOPE
            
Mathematical Foundations of Balance & Quantum Coherence

            

              My research lies at the nexus of analytical number theory, mathematical physics, and quantum information. Specifically, it investigates pair balance symmetries, the distribution and spectral properties of the nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function, Wigner positivity/negativity of Riemann states, and their realizations in quantum circuits, ternary weight networks, and excitable cortical architectures.
