6-Layer Simplicial Topology

The complex couples two dual 18-node structures across six alternating horizontal planes. Each plane houses an equilateral triangle with $C_3$ rotational symmetry. Consecutive layers undergo spatial inversion ($\theta \to -\theta$), establishing alternating angular sets $[-90^\circ, 30^\circ, 150^\circ]$ and $[90^\circ, 210^\circ, 330^\circ]$.

$$\theta_k^{(l)} = \theta_k^{(0)} + \pi \cdot (l \bmod 2) + \Delta \theta_{\text{shear}}(y, t)$$
02

Hydrodynamic Streamlines & Meniscus

Every vertex in layer $l$ is connected to all edge midpoints of layer $l+1$ via parametric Catmull-Rom streamlines ($90$ cross-filaments total). Rather than rigid chords, intermediate control points bow outward with local fluid vorticity, tracing the direction of mass transport flux through the central throat.

$$\mathbf{P}_{\text{mid}} = \frac{V_{l, j} + M_{l+1, k}}{2} + \mathbf{n}_{\text{vortex}} \cdot d_{\text{bow}}(t)$$

            BIOGRAPHY & RESEARCH SCOPE
            
Mathematical Foundations of Balance & Quantum Coherence

            

              My research lies at the nexus of analytical number theory, mathematical physics, and quantum information. Specifically, it investigates pair balance symmetries, the distribution and spectral properties of the nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function, Wigner positivity/negativity of Riemann states, and their realizations in quantum circuits, ternary weight networks, and excitable cortical architectures.
            


            

              

                
01

                

                  
Riemann Zeros & Signed Currents

                  
Macdonald function expansions, phase-space Wigner averages, and reformulations of the Riemann Hypothesis via the current $J=2\partial_y|\Xi(x+iy)|^2$.

                

              


              

                
02

                

                  
Quantum States & Decoherence

                  
Pure states constructed from positive kernel transforms, Lee-Yang theorems, and exact decoherence dynamics in coupled spin systems.

                

              


              

                
03

                

                  
Exclusion Laws & Prime Clocks

                  
Clock representations of integers coprime to 6, the exclusion product $E=0.88022\dots$, and twin prime recurrence dynamics.

                

              


              

                
04

                

                  
Balanced Networks & Radix Economy

                  
Inward/outward charge cancellation in excitable tissue, and optimality of balanced-ternary arithmetic in neural networks.
