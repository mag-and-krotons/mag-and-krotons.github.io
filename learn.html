Questions

Before you pay

Who will I be learning from?

From me, Abhijit Singh, directly. Nobody else answers for me. My papers and the research record are on the papers page.

Do I need a background in mathematics or physics?

No. Tell me where you're starting from and I'll begin there. Guided readings and sessions go at your pace.

How do I pay?

By UPI, from any app: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or your bank's own app. The payment goes directly to my bank account. There is no card payment for now, and payments are open to people in India only.

How do sessions happen?

On a video call at a time we agree. After you pay and send the reference, I write to you within 24 hours to set the time.

Can I get a refund?

If I can't give you what you paid for, I return the full amount to the same UPI account. If you cancel more than 24 hours before a session, I refund it or move it to another time.
